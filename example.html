Selected work

Projects

A selection of my academic, professional and personal work.

Vulnerability Exploitation Risk Analysis

Prioritizing real-world vulnerability risk by combining severity, exploit likelihood and known-exploited data.

  • CVSS
  • EPSS
  • CISA KEV
  • Risk Analysis
Process & results

Process

Collected vulnerability records and enriched each one with its CVSS severity score, EPSS exploit-probability score and CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) status.

Results

Showed that severity alone is a poor guide: combining all three signals highlights the vulnerabilities that are most likely to be exploited.

Network Design & Traffic Analysis

Designed and configured network environments, then captured and analyzed traffic to understand how devices communicate. (Network Security course)

  • Cisco Packet Tracer
  • Wireshark
  • Networking
Process & results

Process

Built multi-device topologies in Cisco Packet Tracer and generated traffic between hosts. Captured packets with Wireshark and filtered them by protocol.

Results

Mapped normal network behavior and learned to spot unusual communication patterns in packet captures.

Disk & File Examination

Forensic analysis of disk images and file artifacts to examine digital evidence and file structures. (Digital Forensics course)

  • FTK Imager
  • HxD
  • Digital Forensics
Process & results

Process

Mounted and inspected disk images with FTK Imager, then examined file headers and raw bytes in the HxD hex editor.

Results

Identified file types from their signatures and documented the evidence in a forensically sound way.

Statistics Tutoring AI Agent
